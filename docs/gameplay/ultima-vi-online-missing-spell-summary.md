# Ultima VI Online Missing Spell Summary

## Circle 1

- **Detect Magic:** Identifies whether a targeted item is magical and reports its remaining magical charges.

## Circle 2

- **Infravision:** Reveals nearby warm-blooded creatures in darkness without creating light.
- **Reappear:** Restores an object previously removed with Vanish.
- **Trap:** Places a concealed trap on a door or chest. Since enemies never open them, it had little practical use.
- **Vanish:** Temporarily removes a simple object from the world; Reappear reverses it.

## Circle 3

- **Curse:** Reduces one foe's armor effectiveness, combat prowess, and intelligence.
- **Magic Lock:** Magically locks a door or chest. It could also be exploited with Unlock Magic to remove an ordinary lock.
- **Repel Undead:** Makes every undead creature within sight flee.

## Circle 4

- **Animate:** Brings an inanimate object to life. It wanders independently and may retain abilities such as a wand's attack.

## Circle 5

- **Seance:** Allows normal conversation with dead characters, without resurrecting them.

## Circle 6

- **Clone:** Creates an identical but uncontrollable copy of a creature, allied with the original.
- **Confuse:** Causes multiple enemies to lose awareness of the party and stop fighting temporarily.

## Circle 7

- **Fear:** Makes nearby evil enemies flee in terror.
- **Mass Curse:** Applies Curse's penalties to nearby beings, including friends as well as enemies.
- **Wing Strike:** Launches a dragon-wing attack that damages multiple opponents.

## Circle 8

- **Eclipse:** Temporarily darkens the world as though the moons eclipsed the sun; chiefly reduces monsters' sight range.
- **Armageddon:** Irreversibly destroys virtually all life in Britannia except the caster and Lord British, leaving the game unwinnable.
